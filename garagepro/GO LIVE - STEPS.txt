GaragePro v3 — MendTech Mobile — LIVE BUILD
===========================================

Publish this ONLY after you have tested the preview and are happy.
It is the same app as the preview, but uses your real data and cloud sync.

1. BACKUP (1 min)
   Live app > Settings > Backup & data > Download backup.

2. PUBLISH (2 min)
   app.netlify.com > mendtechauto > Deploys > drag THIS folder (GaragePro-v3-Live) onto the upload box.
   Open https://mendtechauto.netlify.app on the office PC and phones (close & reopen once to load v3).
   Your data and settings stay. Everything you had before works the same.

3. SET UP MOBILE (5 min)
   Settings > Mobile & booking: hours, zones & call-out fees, surcharges, van name/plate, services.
   Settings > Technicians: add your driver / mobile technician.
   Settings > Staff & security: link each login to their Technicians entry (for "My jobs").

4. CONNECT THE ONLINE BOOKING PAGE (5 min, once)
   a) Settings > Mobile & booking > "Copy SQL" > Supabase > SQL Editor > paste > Run.
   b) Same screen: "Download config.js" (cloud sync must be signed in).
   c) Put that config.js into this folder's "js" folder (replace the existing one).
   d) Drag this folder onto Netlify Deploys again.
   Your booking page: https://mendtechauto.netlify.app/book.html
   Print the QR poster from Settings > Mobile & booking.

UNDO
   Netlify > Deploys > click the previous deploy > "Publish deploy". Data is not affected.
   The office PC offline copy (C:\Users\S445696\GaragePro) keeps the previous version until you replace it.
