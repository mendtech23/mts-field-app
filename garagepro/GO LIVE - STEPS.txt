WHAT'S NEW IN 3.4 (on top of 3.1 security, 3.2 branding, 3.3 revenue features)
  - Everyone gets their own login: Owner = e-mail + password + 6-digit code from an
    authenticator app on your phone; staff = username + password you create for them.
    The PIN is now only the screen lock on each device.
  - What each role can see and change is enforced by the cloud database, not just hidden:
    Manager, Service Advisor, Technician (own jobs, customer name only, no prices),
    Driver (own mobile jobs, customer name + phone + location, no prices).
  - Voids, deletions, big discounts, payment links, bank details, reopening a month need
    your approval PIN, checked by the server (5 wrong tries lock it and alert you).
  - Audit log of every change that nobody can edit. Devices list: sign out a lost phone at once.
  - Alerts by e-mail to mendtech23@gmail.com (instant for important things, summary at 20:00)
    and in the app (Settings > Staff & security).
  - Customers approve quotations from a link in the WhatsApp message.

NEW IN 3.5
  - Finance > Other income (scrap, car sales, used parts...) - counted in P&L, VAT, month-end.
  - "Plain document" tick on quotations and invoices: no company name, logo or contact details.
