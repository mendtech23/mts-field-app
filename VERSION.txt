MendTech OS v14 (Field Ops)

Zoho One stays the system of record. MendTech OS is the field layer.

NEW IN v14 - OWNER-ONLY MODE
The whole app is gated behind the owner master code while real staff logins
(phone OTP + server-side authorization) are being built on a backend.
Staff who open the app see a locked screen with a button to remove company
data from their device. Flip OWNER_ONLY_MODE to false in app.js once real
authentication is live.

WHY: the role system in v13 is user-interface only. All data sits in
localStorage on every device, so any staff member could read every price,
job, and phone number using browser developer tools. That is not fixable
without a backend - see docs/SECURITY_AND_ROLES.md.

NEW IN v14 - REAL AC READINGS
Per-unit capture: supply/return air temp with auto Delta-T and a cooling
verdict (healthy 8-14C, weak 5-8C, poor under 5C), suction and discharge
pressure, gas type, compressor amps, filter condition, notes.
Owner can mark readings mandatory per job; the job cannot be completed
until at least one unit is recorded. Workers record, only the Owner sets
the requirement. Every reading queues to Zoho.

Roles:

- Admin
